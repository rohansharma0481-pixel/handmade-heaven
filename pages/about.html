Our Story

About Us

We are a small, passionate team that believes in the magic of handmade — every stitch, every brushstroke, every carefully moulded piece of clay tells a story. At Handmade Heaven, we craft unique keychains, wall decor, art pieces, and home accents that you simply won't find anywhere else.

Crafted with Love, Designed for Life

Handmade Heaven was born from a simple belief: that true creativity lives in the hands. Each product in our collection is individually handcrafted — no mass production, no shortcuts. From delicate clay keychains to vibrant wall murals, every piece is a labour of love.

We specialise in personalised gifts, keychains, home decor, fine art sketches, and custom-made products. Whether you are looking for the perfect gift or a unique piece to decorate your space, we have something special made just for you.

Our artisans pour their heart into every creation, ensuring that each item you receive carries warmth, character, and a story worth telling.
